One press. Forty one minutes.

The night Aoife Ní Bhriain went over the side off Mizen Head, and the beacon on her lifejacket brought the lifeboat to her.

Why it worked

  • It was on her, not on the boat

    Saoirse sailed on for another hour. The beacon on Aoife's lifejacket was the only thing that knew where she was.

  • It floated and kept talking

    Antenna up in a two metre swell, it sent a fresh position every five minutes while she drifted 1.3 miles east.

  • It brought them the last mile

    The satellites got the lifeboat to the area. The strobe and the homing signal got it to her.
