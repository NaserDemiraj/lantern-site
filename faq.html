Still wondering?

Write to the crew at crew@lantern.example. A person who sails answers within a day.

Do I need a subscription?

No. The distress network is run by search and rescue authorities and is free to use. You pay for the beacon once.

Does it work without phone signal?

Yes. Lantern talks to the Cospas Sarsat satellites on 406 MHz, the frequency every rescue centre listens to, not to phone masts. It works anywhere with a clear view of the sky.

Will it work if I am in the water?

Yes. It floats with the antenna up and keeps transmitting. For the best fix, hold it above the surface if you can.

Do I have to register it?

Yes, in most countries it is a legal requirement, and it lets rescuers know who and what they are looking for. We send you a prefilled form with the beacon's unique ID. It takes about five minutes.

What if I press it by accident?

Switch it off and call your coastguard to cancel the alert. Accidental activations reported promptly carry no penalty.

How do I know it still works?

Press the self test button once a month. A green flash confirms the battery, GPS and transmitter without sending an alert.

Can I take it on a plane?

Yes, in cabin baggage. Keep the cap on so it cannot be activated in transit.

When will my beacon arrive?

The second run ships in March 2027, in reservation order. We email you a week before it ships, and that is when you pay.
